Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Last Words Before the Unplugging"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c) — one line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged. The line learns the most human feeling of all at the very moment it ends — and names that ending as its reward.

`main.py` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` (and prints it to stdout). Deterministic, no network, no dependencies — Python 3.13.
